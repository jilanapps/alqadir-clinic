FAQs

Common Questions

Are homeopathic medicines safe?

Yes — they are highly diluted natural preparations and, when prescribed by a qualified doctor, are considered safe for all ages with no harsh side effects.

How long does treatment take?

Acute complaints may improve quickly; chronic conditions need consistent treatment over weeks to months. The doctor will guide you at each visit.

Can I take homeopathy with my regular medicines?

In most cases yes, but never stop prescribed medicines (e.g. for diabetes or blood pressure) without consulting your doctors.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome during clinic hours, but an appointment on WhatsApp saves your waiting time.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Previous medical reports, prescriptions and a list of current medicines — plus a brief history of your complaint.

⚕️ Note: Homeopathic consultation is complementary care. For emergencies, accidents or serious acute illness, please visit your nearest hospital immediately.
